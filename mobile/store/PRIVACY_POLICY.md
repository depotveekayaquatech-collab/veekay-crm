## What we collect

| Information | Why |
|---|---|
| Name, email address (your login), phone number | To create and run your account and contact you about deliveries |
| The stores linked to your account | To show you only your own stores' information |
| Tickets, replies, delivery confirmations you submit | To resolve delivery problems |
| Photos and documents you choose to attach or upload (for example a photo of damaged bottles, a payment proof) | As evidence for tickets and compliance records |
| A notification token for your phone, if you allow notifications | To send ticket updates |
| For Veekay employees: your location, only at the moment you tap Check in or Check out (never in the background), and the bottle codes you scan, orders you mark, leave you request and cash purchases (amount, bill photo) you record | Attendance, bottle tracking and expense records |
| A record of actions in the app (sign-ins, tickets, uploads) | Security and an audit trail |

We do **not** track your location in the background, collect your contacts or payment card details, and we do not use advertising identifiers. Location is read only when an employee checks in or out, and the app works without it.

Photos attached to tickets and deliveries are re-saved without their camera and location metadata before we store them.

## How it is used and shared

We use your information only to provide the service described above. We do not sell it. It is processed on our servers and our
cloud providers [hosting provider, storage provider]. Notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging.
We share information with Veekay staff and, for tickets and deliveries at a store, with the vendors and store contacts linked to that store.
We may disclose information where the law requires it.

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (https). Sign-in tokens are kept in the phone's secure storage. Accounts lock after repeated wrong passwords.

use **Account → Request data deletion** in the app, or email [email]. We will respond within [30] days; records we are legally required to keep are retained.
