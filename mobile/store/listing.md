**App name** (30 chars max): Veekay Partner

**Short description** (80 chars max):
Orders, deliveries and tickets for Veekay vendors and store contacts.

**Full description** (4000 chars max):

Veekay Partner keeps vendors and store contacts (POCs) in step with Veekay on every delivery.

For store POCs
• Confirm each day's delivery, or flag one that arrived short or damaged, with photos
• Raise a ticket in seconds and follow the conversation with the Veekay team
• Upload compliance cards and payment proofs straight from your phone camera

For vendors
• See bottles delivered to each of your stores, day by day
• Follow every ticket and delivery report your store POCs raise
• Track bills and due dates, and open your documents
• See which bottles are currently at your stores

For Veekay employees
• Check in and out with your location, apply for leave
• Mark orders for many dates at once
• Scan bottle QR codes IN and OUT, even without a connection
• Record cash purchases with a photo of the bill

For everyone
• Alerts when a ticket changes or the team replies
• Works with the login Veekay gave you; no sign-up needed

Veekay Partner is for existing Veekay vendors and store contacts. Your login is created by Veekay. If you do not have one, please contact your Veekay representative.
